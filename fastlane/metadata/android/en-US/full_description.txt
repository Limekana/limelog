LimeLog is a periodized strength-training tracker for serious lifters.

Build training programs with phases, log every set and rep, and let LimeLog handle the analysis:

* Automatic personal-record detection (Epley 1RM) after each session
* Progressive-overload reference — last session's sets shown while you log
* Estimated 1RM trends and a per-exercise personal-record history
* Optional vertical-jump and body-metric tracking
* Health Connect steps and active calories

Your data stays on your device. Cloud sync is optional and account-gated. No ads, no trackers, no Google Play Services — LimeLog is built to be fully open and F-Droid friendly.

Part of the Limecore Nexus suite.
