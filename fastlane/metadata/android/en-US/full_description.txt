LimeLog is a periodized strength-training tracker.

Features:
* Build programs with phases
* Log every set and rep
* Auto-detect personal records (Epley 1RM)
* Progressive-overload reference while logging
* Estimated 1RM trends with a per-exercise PR history
* Optional vertical-jump and body-metric tracking
* Health Connect steps and active calories

Local-first; optional account-gated cloud sync. No Google Play Services, no Firebase — Google sign-in is a plain web OAuth flow.

Part of the Limecore Nexus suite. Available in English, Finnish, French, German, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Portuguese, Indonesian and Arabic.
